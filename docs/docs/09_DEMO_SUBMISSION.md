## One-paragraph submission description (draft)

BACKSTOP is an agent-native recovery-readiness simulator for small digital teams. It lets a team map how its critical online services depend on accounts, administrators, devices and recovery methods, then simulate losing any of those resources. A deterministic graph engine explains which capabilities are blocked, which independent routes remain available and where recovery loops or unverified assumptions exist. The integrated agent operates on the same live map, simulation records and remediation tasks as the user, helping them strengthen their plans and generate a usable emergency runbook—without storing credentials or connecting to live accounts.
